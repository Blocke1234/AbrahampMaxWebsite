## 2. Design System & Rationale

The site was built to feel expensive, exclusive, serious, and earned. It uses restraint over decoration, and silence as a design element.

### Visual Tokens
- **Palette:** Ink (`#0A0A08`), Ink-Soft (`#12120F`), Bone (`#EDEAE2`), Bone-Dim (`#A8A399`), Bone-Faint (`#837E73`), and Gold (`#C6A15B`).
- **Typography:** Libre Caslon Text (Display, editorial serif) and Inter (Body/UI).
- **Gold Ration Rule:** The gold accent is strictly rationed. It appears only on the primary CTA, kickers, and serial numbers. Scarcity of the accent mirrors the scarcity of the offer.

### Answers to the Brief's Seven Questions

**Q1 · Hero:**
I led with a typographic statement rather than a face. A premium, editorial serif setting "From lost to locked-in." reads more expensive than a mediocre corporate headshot. It sets the tone immediately. I have provided a shot list below so you can swap in a proper portrait later.

**Q2 · Credibility without bragging:**
The receipts on the About page are typeset as a "service record" ledger. Each fact is paired with a one-line annotation of what it cost or proves (e.g., "Earned, not inherited."). It reads like a dossier, not a dashboard.

**Q3 · Scarcity that stays honest:**
The 5-seat cap is presented as a static, typeset line: "Five seats. Then the door closes." The mechanism (1-on-1 calls and daily texts) is explained clearly, so the cap justifies itself without needing fake countdown timers.

**Q4 · $199 without feeling cheap:**
The price sits inside a gold-hairline framed offer card, presented *after* the structure of the program is laid out. It is labeled "Founding-cohort pricing — before the ladder climbs." Context comes before the number.

**Q5 · Motion:**
Motion is limited to exactly two moments: a single staggered fade-up on the hero headline on load (CSS only, respects reduced-motion preferences), and a slow 500ms ease on CTA hovers. Everything else is still, because stillness reads as expensive.

**Q6 · Credibility without testimonials:**
Credibility is established through the first-person voice (one man speaking directly, not a company), the extreme specificity of the mechanism (1–3 calls/week, daily texts, 30 days), and the restraint of the design itself.

**Q7 · Mobile Priority:**
The mobile flow is ruthless: Hook + CTA → Pain → Proof → Mechanism → Offer → Objections → Close. A sticky bottom CTA bar appears after the hero section and hides near the footer, ensuring "Book a Call" is always one tap away.

## 3. Asset Shot List (For Future Updates)

When you are ready to add photography, drop the files into the `/public/media/` folder. Here is the exact spec needed to maintain the premium feel:

1. **Hero Portrait:** True Black & White (or heavily desaturated). Face and upper body, hard directional light, plain background. Must look serious and earned.
2. **Candids:** 2–3 vertical shots (training, working, or lived-in moments) to intersperse on the About page.
3. **Video (Optional):** A 5–10 second silent, high-quality loop for the hero background, provided it doesn't distract from the typography.
